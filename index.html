📐 Mathematical & Astrophotographic Forensic Methodology

AstroSessionDoctor applies rigorous optical transfer function and astrometric point spread function (PSF) models to pinpoint the root cause of image degradation:

Gaussian Point Spread Function & FWHM:
$$\text{FWHM} = 2 \sqrt{2 \ln 2} \cdot \sigma \approx 2.35482 \cdot \sigma$$ Relates the standard deviation $\sigma$ of the star flux profile to the measured Full Width at Half Maximum in arcseconds.
Stellar Eccentricity & Axis Ratio:
$$e = \sqrt{1 - \frac{b^2}{a^2}} = \sqrt{1 - \frac{\sigma_{\min}^2}{\sigma_{\max}^2}}$$ Values of $e < 0.35$ indicate circular diffraction-limited stars. $e > 0.45$ signifies tracking drift, optical coma, or severe wind buffeting.
Total Guiding RMS (PHD2 Error Vector):
$$\text{RMS}_{\text{total}} = \sqrt{\text{RMS}_{\text{RA}}^2 + \text{RMS}_{\text{Dec}}^2}$$ Acceptable astrophotographic guiding requires $\text{RMS}_{\text{total}} \le 0.5 \times \text{PixelScale}$.
Critical Focus Zone (CFZ):
$$\text{CFZ} = \pm 4.88 \cdot \lambda \cdot f_{\#}^2 \quad (\mu\text{m})$$ Calculates the depth of field before defocus induces a wave-front error exceeding $\lambda/4$ (Strehl ratio $< 0.80$).
